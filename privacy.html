Pop Parade Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026 · Applies to the Pop Parade Android app and hosted web game

Who operates the game?

Pop Parade is provided by the project owner saidk84 (Pop Parade project). For privacy questions, use the project's contact/issue page; please do not post personal information in a public issue.

What information is used?

No name, email, age, account, address, precise location or contact list is requested. The game saves level completion, scores, stickers, selected preferences and sound settings locally on the player's device, using local browser/WebView storage. No gameplay data is transferred to the game developer, sold or shared for advertising or analytics.

Ads, permissions and third parties

The Android app contains no ads, in-app purchases, analytics, third-party advertising SDKs or tracking SDKs. The Android app does not request the Internet, location, camera, microphone or contacts permission. Its HTML game is bundled inside the app, not downloaded from an external website. Android spoken hints, if enabled, use the device's installed text-to-speech service. Its provider's privacy practices may apply to voice processing.

Storage and deletion

Game progress and preferences remain locally until they are reset using the game's settings, cleared via Android app settings, or removed by uninstalling the app. The developer has no server copy to delete. The standalone web version may also store game progress in the local storage of the browser; the browser's own privacy and storage settings apply.

Children and families

Pop Parade includes gentle kids' play and a separate higher-difficulty arcade mode. No user registration is required and no child personal information is requested. Parents can turn off spoken instructions in the game's settings.

Changes and inquiries

If the app's data practices change, this policy and the applicable Google Play disclosures should be updated before release. For questions, open a repository issue without posting sensitive personal information.
