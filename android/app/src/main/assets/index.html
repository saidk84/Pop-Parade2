Pop Parade privacy policy

For families and players: Pop Parade is an offline game for kids, teens and adults. No account is required.

Data on your device: Your level progress, scores, stickers and sound settings stay in local storage on this device. They are not sent to the Pop Parade developer or a game server. You can delete them using the game's reset option or by clearing the app's data/uninstalling it.

No advertising or tracking: This app has no ads, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking SDKs, and requests no Internet, location, contacts or microphone permission. It does not ask children for names, ages or email addresses.

Spoken instructions: If enabled, spoken hints use your device's configured text-to-speech engine. The engine's provider may have its own privacy practices. You can turn off spoken instructions in the grown-up settings.

Privacy questions: Contact the developer through the project's GitHub Issues page; please do not post sensitive personal details publicly. Privacy information is also available on the project's public website.

Changes: Any future data collection or advertising would require updating this notice and relevant Play disclosures. Updated November 11, 2026.
